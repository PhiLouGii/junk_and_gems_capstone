## 2. Discussion

### 2.1 Importance of Milestones

**Milestone 1: Requirement Analysis**  
Surveys, interviews, and literature review identified gaps in Maseru’s waste management system and artisan economy. Findings guided the app’s design and feature selection.

**Milestone 2: MVP Design & Development**  
Developed the Flutter mobile app for donors, artisans, and buyers, and the React-based web dashboard for administrators. Connected both to a Node.js/PostgreSQL backend.

**Milestone 3: Core Functionalities Implementation**  
Implemented waste posting, donor-artisan matching, user profiles, gamification, and multi-platform access. All objectives were successfully delivered.

**Milestone 4: Testing and Evaluation**  
Tested the app across different devices and data scenarios. Confirmed core functionalities, app stability, and usability. Minor performance adjustments were made to optimize the experience.

**Milestone 5: Pilot Deployment**  
Deployed the platform to a controlled group of users in Maseru. Collected preliminary feedback and usage data to evaluate engagement, waste diversion, and system performance.
